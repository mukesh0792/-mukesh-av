How to use your resume

Choose the version matching the vacancy. Each version uses a clear single-column, text-first layout without photos, progress bars, salary or unverified achievements.

Open the resume and select Print / Save as PDF. In the browser print dialogue, choose Save as PDF (or the available PDF printer). Attach that PDF to a role-specific application.

For a specific vacancy, tailor the job title and relevant technical keywords to the actual posting without claiming experience you cannot evidence. Work authorisation and other application-specific details should be confirmed separately.
