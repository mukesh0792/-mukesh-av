INDEPENDENT DIGITAL CINEMA INTELLIGENCE

Cinema AI

An independently developed, cinema-focused AI diagnostic system designed to help engineers investigate equipment failures using technical logs, curated service knowledge and evidence-based troubleshooting.

The goal is a specialized engineering system: identify the exact equipment and symptoms, retrieve model-relevant information, distinguish observed facts from possible causes, and suggest checks that a qualified engineer can validate. It is designed to improve through verified field outcomes and approved knowledge updates—not by accepting unverified answers as truth.

Technical evidence → Model-aware retrieval → Engineer review

Development foundation

  • Diagnostic workflow and curated cinema knowledge-base foundation
  • Scope across Barco, Christie and NEC projectors; Dolby audio and IMS; GDC and other cinema playback systems
  • Early log-analysis, evidence handling and retrieval components under test

Pending engineering work

  • Improve manual ingestion, including tables, scanned pages and source provenance
  • Verify exact projector model, hardware revision and firmware relevance
  • Strengthen page-level citations, quote fidelity and grounded diagnosis
  • Add controlled technical web research with trustworthy-source checks
  • Validate engineer-feedback learning, regression tests, rollback and safety controls
  • Complete realistic performance, security and end-to-end acceptance testing

REGIONAL PROJECTOR PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Regional Projector PPM Bot

A region-focused automation initiative to organize projector preventive-maintenance information and develop richer, more consistent engineer-reviewed PPM reports.

The existing workflow foundation supports site-oriented status and review of verified maintenance records. The next reporting layer aims to turn approved field observations into clear maintenance summaries, findings, corrective-action records and follow-up recommendations—without inventing measurements or bypassing engineer approval.

Established foundation

  • Rajasthan-specific PPM workflow and structured site/report review
  • Verified-data handling and controlled draft/approval design
  • Separation of maintenance evidence from unapproved communications

Enhanced reporting roadmap

  • Generate standardized, more informative projector PPM report drafts from validated evidence
  • Improve comparison of observations, identified risks, maintenance actions and open follow-ups
  • Verify report accuracy, approval controls and delivery workflow before broader use
